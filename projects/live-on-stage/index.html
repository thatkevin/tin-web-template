The programme

Making musicians

Live on Stage is The Tin's youth music programme for instrumentalists aged 11–18 at Grade 4 and above. Young musicians are brought together to form bands, collaborate on original songs, and perform live.

Sessions run on Monday evenings at the Coal Vaults. At the end of each term, bands perform a showcase concert on the Tin's stage - often for the first time.

The programme costs £80 per term - deliberately kept low because we believe access to music shouldn't depend on your family's income.

Who it's for

  • → Instrumentalists aged 11–18
  • → Grade 4 or above on your instrument
  • → Any instrument welcome
  • → All genres - rock, pop, folk, jazz, more

Practicalities

  • → Mondays, The Coal Vaults
  • → £80 per term
  • → Bursaries available - ask us
