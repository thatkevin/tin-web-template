We don't use cookies

The Tin Music and Arts website does not use cookies to track you. We do not use advertising networks, retargeting pixels, or third-party tracking of any kind. You will not find a cookie consent banner here because there is nothing to consent to.

What we collect and why

We collect minimal data, only what's needed to run the website:

  • Contact form submissions - name, email address, and your message. We use this to respond to your enquiry. We don't add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent.
  • Newsletter sign-ups - your email address, if you choose to sign up. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.
  • Server logs - our web host automatically records IP addresses and page requests for security purposes. These are not shared with anyone and are deleted periodically.

Third parties

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Maps - the map on our Find Us page is embedded from Google. Google's own privacy policy applies to any data they collect.
  • Ticket providers - if you click through to buy tickets, you are visiting a third-party site. Their privacy policy applies.

Your rights
